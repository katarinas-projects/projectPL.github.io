PROJECT PL / ABOUT

Why Project PL exists

Project PL is a student-led research club at Prior Lake High School.

Our purpose

We ask questions about our school, gather evidence, interview members of the community, analyze data, and publish our findings in a way that is understandable and useful to students.

How we work

Our goal is to separate verified information from assumptions and clearly explain our sources. When information is uncertain or incomplete, we say so.

Who can participate?

Project PL is for students interested in journalism, law, research, interviewing, data, school policy, or simply understanding the place where they spend their days.
